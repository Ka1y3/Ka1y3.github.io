ClipForge Privacy Policy

ClipForge runs locally on the user's own computer.

Data processed: Twitch and TikTok access tokens, video files and clip metadata (titles, descriptions, hashtags).

Storage: all data is stored locally on the user's computer; tokens are stored encrypted. Nothing is sent to ClipForge servers because there are none.

Sharing: data is only sent to Twitch, TikTok or YouTube when the user requests it, in order to connect accounts or publish a clip. We do not sell or share data with third parties.

Deletion: the user can disconnect accounts in the app settings and delete all data by deleting the application's data folder. TikTok access can also be revoked from the TikTok app settings.
