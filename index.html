ClipForge

ClipForge is a personal tool that turns a streamer's Twitch VODs into short vertical clips and lets the streamer publish them to their own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

Features: automatic detection of the best moments, clip editing (9:16, 16:9, 1:1), automatic subtitles, scheduled publishing.

ClipForge runs locally on the user's computer and is used by its developer to manage their own content.
